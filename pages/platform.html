How Onboarding Works

Create the church here, then send its sign-up link to the first admin there. The first person who registers using that link should be manually promoted to Super Admin — open the Members tab in their Admin Dashboard is not accessible until you do this in Firestore directly (set their role field to superAdmin). From there, that church's own admin manages everything else — departments, meetings, members — without ever seeing other churches.
